Company 01

AfriVid Studio

Visit AfriVid Studio →

Overview

AfriVid Studio is an AI-powered video creation platform built for African creators, teachers, and business owners. A user types a topic, and the platform automatically generates a complete, ready-to-share video — script, voice narration, visuals, and editing — without needing separate tools for each step. Its core differentiator is native voice dubbing in African languages — Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, Arabic, French, and Portuguese — built into the generation pipeline from the start, not added afterward as a translation layer.

Founded by Regan Odhiambo Ayiecho, who lived this problem firsthand as a YouTube creator jumping between Synthesia, ElevenLabs, Canva, and CapCut to produce a single video.

One-Pager
AfriVid Studio
Africa’s AI Video Platform — Built for African Languages
The Problem

Creating a single video means jumping between four or five disconnected platforms — scripting, voice, visuals, editing — wasting hours creators could spend on income-generating work. Worse, most AI video tools are built English-first, leaving native-language African audiences excluded from content meant to reach them.

The Solution

AfriVid Studio collapses the entire video production chain into one AI-powered platform. Type a topic — AfriVid writes the script, generates the voice, builds the visuals, and assembles a complete, ready-to-share video automatically, with native voice dubbing in Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, Arabic, French, and Portuguese built into the pipeline from the start.

Traction

111 active users, organic growth, $0 marketing spend. Launched June 2026. Real, repeat usage — not one-time signups.

Business Model

Free: $0/month, 3 videos, all 6 languages. Pro: $1/month, unlimited videos, no watermark. Organization licensing — in development.

Market

Africa Creator Economy Market: ~$5.1B (2025) — $29.8B projected by 2032. Zero direct competitors offer native African-language voice dubbing today.

Company 02

Jua Institute

Visit Jua Institute →

Overview

Jua Institute is an AI-powered learning platform addressing a gap AI itself created. Students now pass exams and coursework using AI without building the underlying skill — a debt that surfaces later in internships, jobs, or courses that assume prior competency.

Two audiences: (1) University students recovering practical skills from their own program’s coursework, through live AI tutor classes, real sandboxes, and AI-graded assignments. (2) Form four leavers — waiting to join university or not planning to — building new, forward-looking skills (Google Workspace, IT fundamentals, AI-era readiness) during their gap year.

Founded by Regan Odhiambo Ayiecho.

Status: Live prototype. Core learning loop fully functional (live AI tutor classes, short videos, sandboxes, AI-graded assignments, human assistance) with automated, personalized learning path generation.

Product

How it works:

  1. Student enters program, university, term, and topics.
  2. System automatically generates a personalized learning path and schedule.
  3. Student receives an email invite once ready.
  4. Learns through live AI tutor classes, short topic videos, real sandboxes (no copy-pasting possible), practical AI-graded assignments, and on-demand human help when stuck.
